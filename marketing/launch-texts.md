# Launch texts – Human Resources – World Wide Workforce

Copy, adjust a few words to sound like you, and post. Replace anything in [brackets].
Poster to print or share as a picture: `poster-A4.pdf` and `poster.png` in this folder.

## 1. Facebook group post (wind, rope access, blade repair, offshore groups)

Ask the group admin first if the group has rules about links.

> Hi all 👋
>
> I've worked in [wind / blade repair / your field] for [X] years and kept seeing the same problem:
> good technicians looking for the next project, and companies who can't find them in time.
>
> So I built **humanresourceswww.com**. It's a free map where technicians and skilled workers
> make a profile (skills, certificates, languages, availability) and employers find them by
> trade and distance to their project.
>
> ✅ Free for workers, no fees ever
> ✅ Your name and contact stay hidden. Only companies I have checked and approved can see them
> ✅ On site, hybrid or home office; employee or B2B/freelance, you choose
> ✅ Beginners welcome, and you can download your profile as a PDF CV
>
> It's new, so every profile helps. If you're open to work (now or later), it takes 2 minutes:
> 👉 https://humanresourceswww.com
>
> Feedback is very welcome, just comment or message me.
