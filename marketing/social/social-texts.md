## Company details (same everywhere)

- **Name:** Human Resources – World Wide Workforce
- **Website:** https://humanresourceswww.com
- **Email:** info@humanresourceswww.eu
- **Category / industry:** Employment services (LinkedIn: "Staffing and Recruiting"; Facebook: "Employment agency")
- **Location:** do not add a street address. Country only, if a field is required.

**Tagline (LinkedIn, max 120 characters):**
> Technicians and skilled workers worldwide on one map: near your project or ready to travel.

**Short description (Facebook "Intro", max 255 characters):**
> Free profiles for technicians and skilled workers worldwide. Companies find people near their project or ready to travel, available now or open to offers. Wind, construction, energy, oil & gas and more. 👉 humanresourceswww.com

**Long description (LinkedIn "About", Facebook "About"):**
> Human Resources – World Wide Workforce puts skilled technicians and workers from all over the world on one map.
>
> Companies find the right people close to their project, or people ready to travel to wherever the work is. They search by trade, skills, certificates (GWO, IRATA, SPRAT and more), availability, right to work and distance to the site.
>
> Workers register for free. They show only their town, never their address, and their name and contact details are visible only to companies we have checked and approved. People who are currently employed can stay in the network as "open to offers", with the date their contract ends.
>
> Trades include wind energy, construction, electrical, welding, plant and lifting, shipbuilding, oil & gas onshore and offshore, factory work and general work. Beginners are welcome.
>
> Free for workers. Free for employers during launch.
> https://humanresourceswww.com
